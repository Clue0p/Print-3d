Seamless Process

How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

01

Upload your CAD Design

Send us your STL, STEP, or OBJ model through our website, WhatsApp, or email. We verify model slice geometry for printability.

02

Select Material & Infill

Choose PLA (₹12/g), PETG (₹18/g), or TPU (₹18/g). Pick layer height (0.12mm to 0.28mm) and infill density according to strength needs.

03

Precision Print & Express Dispatch

We print on calibrated high-precision FDM 3D printers, inspect part dimensions, remove support structures, and ship to your doorstep.
